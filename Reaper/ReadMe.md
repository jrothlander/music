These are a few Reaper projects with a few simple runs on the piano with some old 80s and 90s songs I remember. These are just for me to verify my timing and errors, as well as see if I actually remember how to play them. I want to see what the sheet music generation looks like in Reaper from these.  

As an example, _Endless Love_ is WAY TOO FAST. But it lets me figure out how much I need to slow it down. The other songs all have issues, but that is the point. See what they sound like and what to clean up. Then I can go back and record a better version.  

I will continue to re-record these and clean them up, and try to make them better. I might every try to find the sheet music and play the whole song. But for now, these are just a few simple test runs to see how things sound in Reaper.
