Here are a few Reaper projects with simple piano runs from old 80s and 90s songs that I can remember from memory after about 30 years. These are just for me to verify my timing and errors, as well as see if I actually remember how to play them. I also wanted to see what the sheet music generation looks like in Reaper from these and how other instruments sound like applied to the piano MIDI recordings. 

I started by recording these on the piano and then modified them in Reaper to add orchestral sounds. This is just me playing around a bit. But a few of them sound interesting. 
* _Pirates of the Caribbean_ is a good example of what I am trying to do with these
* _Let It Go_ is interesting
* The Rocky Theme, _Gonna Fly Now_ sounds pretty good 

I have left the original piano recording in for reference. Most of these are pretty bad. _Endless Love_ is WAY TOO FAST. But it lets me figure out how much I need to slow it down. The other songs all have issues, but that is the point. See what they sound like and what to clean up. Then I can go back and record a better version.  

I will continue to re-record these and clean them up, and try to make them better. I might even try to find the sheet music and play the whole song correctly. But for now, these are just a few simple test runs to see how things sound in Reaper.
